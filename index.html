Spectroscopic Classification Study

What is this study about?
This study investigates how different interface designs affect how people classify astronomical spectra.

What will I do?
You will classify 60 spectra into three categories: Star, Galaxy, or Quasar. The session takes approximately 20 minutes.

Do I have to take part?
No. Participation is entirely voluntary. You may close this window at any time without penalty.

Will my data be anonymous?
Yes. No personally identifiable information is collected. Your responses are assigned a random code and stored securely.

What happens to my data?
Data will be used for a Master's dissertation and stored securely for five years before deletion. It will not be shared with third parties.

What is a spectrum?

An astronomical spectrum shows how much light (flux) an object emits at each wavelength. Think of it as a fingerprint: different types of objects have different patterns.
